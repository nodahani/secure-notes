A panda walks into a restaurant, orders a sandwich, eats it, then pulls out a gun and shoots the waiter. As the panda walks out, the manager shouts:
Manager: "Hey! What do you think you're doing?"
Panda: "Look up the word 'panda' in that dictionary."
The manager opens the dictionary and reads: "Panda: A bear-like mammal native to China. Eats, shoots and leaves."
Manager: "Oh, I see... but you shot my waiter!"
Panda: "Exactly. That's what I do. Read the definition again."
